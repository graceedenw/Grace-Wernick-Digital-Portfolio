Program Overview

In May 2027, I will participate in a faculty-led study abroad program through Central Michigan University. This international academic experience will allow me to explore British business culture, professional communication, workplace expectations, and cultural differences between the United Kingdom and the United States.

Through business visits, cultural activities, group discussions, journaling, and experiential learning, I hope to gain a deeper understanding of how cultural perspectives influence business practices and professional relationships.

Anticipated Learning Experiences

International Business Practices

Explore British businesses and organizations and compare their practices with those in the United States.

Cross-Cultural Communication

Develop an understanding of communication styles and professional expectations across cultures.

Collaboration & Teamwork

Participate in group discussions, shared learning experiences, and collaborative activities.

Research & Business Writing

Record observations and complete a professional business report using APA style.

Cultural Intelligence

Develop greater confidence and adaptability when navigating unfamiliar cultural environments.

Professional Growth

Reflect on international experiences and their potential applications to my future career.

Why This Experience Matters to Me

As an Accounting major with a minor in Information Systems, I recognize the importance of understanding how businesses operate across different cultural and professional environments. Through this experience, I hope to strengthen my intercultural communication skills, broaden my understanding of international business, and develop the adaptability necessary to succeed in an increasingly interconnected professional world.
