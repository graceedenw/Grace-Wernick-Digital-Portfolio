My academic journey began at Montcalm Community College, where I earned an Associate of Science & Arts degree and a Liberal Studies certificate following the Michigan Transfer Agreement. I graduated with high honors and a cumulative GPA of 3.97. These accomplishments established a strong academic foundation for continuing my education at Central Michigan University.

My interest in accounting stems from my natural affinity for numbers and appreciation for the structure, clarity, and logic they provide. I am also pursuing a minor in Information Systems because I recognize the increasingly important role technology plays in accounting and modern business.

Throughout my time at Central Michigan University, I hope to expand my knowledge, strengthen my professional skills, and explore the various opportunities within the accounting profession.
