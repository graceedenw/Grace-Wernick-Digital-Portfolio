Project overview

This project involved applying foundational accounting principles to Kelly Consulting, a service-based business. Using Microsoft Excel, I completed general ledger accounts and an end-of-period worksheet, organizing financial transactions, adjustments, account balances, and financial information throughout the accounting cycle.

Skills demonstrated

  • Microsoft Excel
  • General ledger posting
  • Debit and credit classification
  • Adjusting entries
  • Trial balance preparation
  • Financial data organization
  • End-of-period financial reporting
  • Numerical accuracy and attention to detail

Why I included this project

I included this project because it demonstrates my foundational understanding of accounting and my ability to apply classroom concepts through Microsoft Excel. As an Accounting major, I believe this project represents an important step in developing the technical, analytical, and organizational skills needed for my future career.

Personal reflection

Completing this project helped me strengthen my understanding of how individual financial transactions contribute to a business's overall financial position. It also reinforced the importance of accuracy, organization, and attention to detail throughout the accounting process. Working with financial information in Excel allowed me to develop practical skills that I can continue building upon throughout my accounting education.
