Project Overview

This individual Unessay project explores the role of language and communication in conflict, with a particular focus on World War I and World War II. Rather than relying solely on a traditional research essay, I combined written research, a visual presentation, speaking notes, and an audience handout to communicate my findings through multiple formats.

Skills Demonstrated

Public Speaking Academic Research Visual Communication Written Communication Critical Thinking Presentation Design Audience Awareness Project Planning Information Synthesis Microsoft PowerPoint

Why I Included This Project

I included this project because it demonstrates my ability to communicate complex information through multiple formats. Although the subject is historical, the skills involved are directly applicable to business and accounting. Professionals must be able to organize detailed information, present findings clearly, and adapt their communication to different audiences. This project highlights my ability to combine research and analysis with effective written, oral, and visual communication.

Personal Reflection

Completing this project allowed me to develop my communication skills beyond traditional academic writing. Creating a presentation, speaking notes, and an audience handout required me to consider how information could be organized and communicated in different ways. This experience connects to the communication skills I continue to develop in my business coursework, particularly the importance of preparation, clarity, organization, and audience awareness when presenting complex information.
