Project overview

This research project examines how multilingualism influences the academic, social, and cultural experiences of international students studying in the United States. Through a literature review and an original survey, I explored the benefits of multilingualism, language maintenance practices, and the challenges students encounter in an English-dominant environment.

Why I included this project

I selected this project because it demonstrates my ability to conduct independent research, evaluate information from multiple sources, collect original data, and communicate findings in a structured academic document. These skills are valuable in both my academic studies and my future career in accounting and business.

Personal reflection

Completing this project allowed me to strengthen my research, analytical, and written communication skills. Designing a survey and interpreting participants' responses helped me understand the importance of asking thoughtful questions, recognizing patterns, and acknowledging research limitations. I also gained a greater appreciation for the role communication plays in connecting individuals from different linguistic and cultural backgrounds.
