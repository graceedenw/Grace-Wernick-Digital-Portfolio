Project overview

This synthesis essay explores the importance of healthy communication between parents and children when a parent experiences a serious illness. By combining scholarly research with personal experiences, I examined the potential emotional and social effects of parental illness on children and the benefits of open, thoughtful communication. The essay advocates for greater access to reliable guidelines and real-life experiences that can help families navigate difficult conversations.

Why I included this project

I selected this project because it demonstrates my ability to combine information from multiple sources with personal experiences to develop a meaningful and well-supported argument. It also highlights my ability to approach complex and sensitive subjects thoughtfully. The skills demonstrated in this essay, particularly evaluating evidence, organizing information, and communicating clearly, are valuable in both academic and professional business environments.

Personal reflection

Completing this essay gave me the opportunity to explore a topic that holds personal significance while developing my research and written communication skills. I learned how to connect ideas from different sources, incorporate personal experiences into an academic argument, and communicate a message with both evidence and empathy. This assignment reinforced the importance of considering an audience's needs and perspectives when communicating complex information, a skill that remains relevant as I continue my education in accounting and business.
