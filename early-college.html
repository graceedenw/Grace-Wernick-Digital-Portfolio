Academic Growth

Completing college coursework through the Early College program taught me the importance of time management, personal responsibility, and consistent academic effort. Balancing my education with employment helped me develop the independence and discipline necessary to transition successfully into university life. This experience continues to influence my commitment to academic excellence at Central Michigan University.
