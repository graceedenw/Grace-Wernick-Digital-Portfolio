Project Overview

As part of the Teamwork in Business assignment in BIS101WI, my team developed and delivered a 15-minute interactive micro-workshop focused on creating effective business meeting agendas. Our workshop introduced the essential components of a well-organized agenda and guided participants through a practical activity involving a campus fundraising event. The project combined professional business writing, collaborative planning, oral communication, and active audience participation.

My Individual Contributions

  • Created and designed the team's complete PowerPoint presentation.
  • Led the interactive portion of the workshop, guiding participants through the process of developing a meeting agenda.
  • Developed scenario prompts and refined practical examples to support participant understanding.
  • Printed agenda templates for participants to complete during the workshop.
  • Helped organize the workshop content and timing to meet the 15-minute presentation requirement.

Why I Included This Project

I included this project because it demonstrates my ability to apply business communication concepts in a collaborative and practical setting. As an accounting student, I recognize that professional success requires more than technical knowledge. Accountants must also communicate effectively, collaborate with colleagues, organize information, and present complex concepts clearly. This project highlights my ability to take initiative, contribute to a team, and guide an audience through a structured learning experience.

Personal Reflection

This experience helped me recognize the importance of balancing individual initiative with shared responsibility in a team environment. Through developing the presentation and leading the interactive activity, I strengthened my leadership, organization, and public speaking skills. I also gained a greater understanding of how team development, clear expectations, delegation, and communication influence the success of a collaborative project. Moving forward, I hope to continue developing my ability to distribute responsibilities effectively, establish clear expectations, and encourage meaningful participation from all team members.
